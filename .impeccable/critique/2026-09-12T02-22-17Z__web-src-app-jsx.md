## Priority Issues

**[P1] The carousel stands between the searcher and the register, and it is a 14-stop keyboard detour**
- Why: PRODUCT.md judges by time-to-right-page. A dozen Tabs ends on the fifth dot; first row at 1,185px on mobile; shuffled pool.
- Fix: dots as one `role="tablist"` with arrow keys and `tabindex="-1"`, or drop dots (Prev/Next/Pause = 3 stops); pool 10 → 5, newest-first; on `max-width: 480px` render after the first date group or hide.
- Command: /impeccable distill

**[P1] No URL state for search or filter**
- Why: sharing is success metric three; Back from a person page loses the query.
- Fix: mirror query to `?q=` and filter to `?month=|letter=|town=|home=` with `history.replaceState`; read in initial state; post to parent via `lib/frame.js`.
- Command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Sponsor logos interrupt the search → results path**
- Why: logos sit between the count line and results in every state; footer card already shows them full size; ~150px on mobile.
- Fix: move masthead logos above the ink rule as a "presented by" line at 28px, or right of the count line at 24px; keep footer card.
- Command: /impeccable layout

**[P2] Browse panel exposes 42 controls at once; month row includes data ghosts**
- Why: 499px panel on a phone; "February 2025 · 1" chips; absent letters at 1.2:1 read as gaps.
- Fix: cap chips at 6 months, hide months with < 3 records from chips; absent letters `var(--muted)` at 45% or omitted; Town/Funeral home into a second tier.
- Command: /impeccable distill

**[P3] Small-target and small-type leftovers**
- Why: Pause 55×22px and footer index link 13.6px (under 24px floor); tagline/sponsor label 10.5px (under DESIGN.md's 11px floor); colophon ~112 ch; native blue ×.
- Fix: `.featured__pause { min-height: 28px; padding: 6px 10px }`; `.footer__index a { display:inline-block; padding: 8px 0 }`; 11px labels; `.footer__lines { max-width: 72ch }`; style `::-webkit-search-cancel-button`.
- Command: /impeccable polish
